What this app is

KitchanaClipper is a personal, non-commercial tool. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to publish videos the owner has made and approved to the owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to, sold to or used by anyone else.

YouTube

KitchanaClipper uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The app follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

How it is used

  • Nothing is published until the owner approves each video.
  • On TikTok, videos are sent to the owner's inbox as drafts; the owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app.
  • Content follows the rules of each platform (YouTube, Meta and TikTok terms and community guidelines). Videos with AI narration are labelled as AI-generated where the platform allows.
