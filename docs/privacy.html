Privacy Policy – KitchanaClipper

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What this app is

KitchanaClipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to YouTube channels the owner controls. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the signed-in channel's name, so the app can confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

How the data is stored and used

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google's servers.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties.
  • The app does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local tokens/ folder also removes the stored sign-in.

Contact
