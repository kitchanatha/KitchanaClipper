Privacy Policy – KitchanaClipper

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What this app is

KitchanaClipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish short videos the owner has made and approved to YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts the owner controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google user data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the signed-in channel's name, so the app can confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Facebook (Meta) data it accesses

When the owner logs in with Facebook, the app requests pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts: to find the owner's own Page, confirm it is the right Page, and publish the owner's approved videos to it as Reels.

TikTok data it accesses

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app requests user.info.basic (to confirm it is the right account) and video.upload (to send the owner's approved video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft; the owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app).

How the data is stored and used

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are sent only to the platform that issued them (Google, Meta or TikTok).
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.
  • The app does not use platform data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • The app does not read other people's profiles, messages, comments or followers.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Facebook in Settings > Business integrations, TikTok in Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the local tokens/ folder also removes the stored sign-ins.
